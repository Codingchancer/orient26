TUNI account

One username and password for almost everything: email, Moodle, the intranet, Wi-Fi, printing, computer classrooms and Pakki. The account is managed at id.tuni.fi, which is also where I applied for my homepages user right.

  • Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) as soon as the account is active.
  • The password must be changed every 210 days in id.tuni.fi.
