Annual registration

Every academic year I must register as attending or non-attending. If I forget, I lose my right to study and have to apply separately to get it back.

Credits and planning

  • Studies are measured in ECTS credits; 1 credit ≈ 27 hours of student work.
  • A full-time year is about 60 credits. A Bachelor of Engineering degree is 240 credits (about 4 years).
  • I make a personal study plan to track which courses I take and when. I also built my own study-plan tracker in Excel (see Excel).
  • Courses are graded 0–5 (0 = fail) or pass/fail.
