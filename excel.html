My study plan in Excel

For the Individual Study Plan task I built a spreadsheet that tracks my courses, credits and graduation requirements. I shared it through OneDrive.

Features I used

FeatureExampleWhat it does
SUM=SUM(C2:C20)Adds up all credits
SUMIF=SUMIF(D2:D20;"Done";C2:C20)Adds credits only for completed courses
COUNTIF=COUNTIF(D2:D20;"Done")Counts completed courses
IF=IF(E2>=240;"Ready";"Not yet")Shows a result depending on a condition
Conditional formattingGreen = done, yellow = in progressColours cells automatically
Tables & filtersFormat as TableSort and filter courses by period or status
ChartsProgress bar / pie chartVisualises credits completed vs. remaining

Finnish Excel uses ; as the separator in formulas, English Excel uses ,.
